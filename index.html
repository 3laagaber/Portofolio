About

Who I am and what I can build for you.

I'm a network engineer with a bachelor's in communication engineering. I like building networks from the ground up: planning the topology, configuring it on Cisco CLI, and testing it in Packet Tracer and GNS3 before anything touches real hardware.

I work with small and medium businesses, clinics and small hospitals, and students or training centers that need labs, designs and troubleshooting. Outside networking, I practice C++ with small projects like a math game and a rock-paper-scissors game.
